# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The RINAMI Project is committed to providing an open, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone.

We welcome contributors regardless of experience, background, profession, nationality, language, or field of study.

Our shared goal is to advance research through constructive observation, structured thinking, and open discussion.

---

## Our Values

We encourage contributors to:

- Respect different perspectives and experiences.
- Discuss ideas with curiosity and professionalism.
- Support evidence-based reasoning.
- Welcome constructive feedback.
- Share knowledge openly and responsibly.
- Recognize that all hypotheses are subject to revision.

Research progresses through collaboration, not competition.

---

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful in all communications.
- Focus discussions on ideas rather than individuals.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Clearly distinguish observation from interpretation.
- Cite sources whenever appropriate.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Intimidation or threats.
- Deliberate misinformation.
- Spam or disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission.

Disagreement is acceptable.

Disrespect is not.

---

## Research Integrity

The RINAMI Project values research integrity.

Contributors should strive to:

- Report observations honestly.
- Avoid fabricating data.
- Clearly identify assumptions.
- Revise conclusions when new evidence becomes available.
- Respect intellectual property and attribution.

The objective is understanding, not proving preconceived ideas.

---

## AI-Assisted Contributions

Artificial Intelligence may be used to support research activities such as:

- Documentation
- Translation
- Summarization
- Structural analysis
- Hypothesis generation

Contributors remain responsible for reviewing AI-generated content before submission.

---

## Community Responsibility

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Encouraging respectful collaboration.
- Reviewing contributions fairly.
- Addressing inappropriate behavior.
- Maintaining a productive research environment.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please open a private communication with the project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed respectfully and handled appropriately.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Project documentation
- Community interactions related to the RINAMI Project

---

## Acknowledgement

By participating in this project, contributors agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help create an inclusive and constructive research community.

---

## Thank You

Thank you for contributing to the RINAMI Project.

Meaningful observation begins with mutual respect.
