# Research Roadmap

> Building an open methodology for transforming human observation into structured knowledge.

---

# Vision

The RINAMI Project explores how human observation can be transformed into reusable knowledge through structured thinking and AI-assisted analysis.

Rather than building another data collection system, the project seeks to establish a research methodology that connects observation, reasoning, and knowledge creation.

---

# Mission

The mission of the RINAMI Project is to:

- Develop reproducible observation methodologies.
- Build reusable knowledge structures.
- Explore human-AI collaborative research.
- Create practical frameworks applicable across multiple disciplines.

# Research Cycle

The RINAMI Project follows a continuous research cycle.

```
Observe
      ↓
Structure
      ↓
Analyze
      ↓
Generate Hypotheses
      ↓
Verify
      ↓
Document
      ↓
Share
      ↓
Observe Again
```

Research is treated as an iterative process rather than a linear sequence.

# Open Research Philosophy

RINAMI is developed as an open research initiative.

Theoretical concepts, methodologies, and selected sample resources are publicly available through this repository.

Commercial resources, including editable templates and production-ready materials, are distributed separately to support sustainable research and continued development.
